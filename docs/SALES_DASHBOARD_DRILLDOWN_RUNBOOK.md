# Sales dashboard and Staff drill-down runbook

## Ownership and purpose

Every employee remains a canonical `Staff` record governed by the workforce and HR domains. Sales is an additional effective category and Sales Profile capability. The management drill-down selects a Staff record only to resolve its authorized Sales Profile; it does not create a second employee master or expose unrelated HR information.

Canonical portal routes are `/sales/management`, `/sales/me`, and `/sales/staff/:staffId`. The compatibility `/sales/performance` route redirects to `/sales/me`. A pure salesperson's internal context home is `/sales/me`.

Canonical API reads are `GET /api/sales-performance/management`, `GET /api/sales-performance/me`, and `GET /api/sales-performance/staff/{staffId}`. `/api/sales/me` remains the distinct authenticated Sales Profile endpoint and must not be repurposed as a performance dashboard.

## Authorization and privacy

- `/sales/me` calls the unfiltered scoped dashboard and never sends or accepts a Staff identifier.
- `/sales/staff/:staffId` calls the dedicated Staff drill-down API and requires `sales.performance.view-team` or `sales.performance.view-all` before central Sales Profile scope is evaluated.
- Resolve the Staff-to-Profile mapping inside the selected legal entity. Multiple matches fail closed and require an explicit legal entity.
- A current team manager may drill only into the current effective team returned by `SalesAccessScope`. Former managers and peers receive the same scoped denial without target data.
- Staff drill-down suppresses company-wide alert suppression evidence even for a view-all actor; it returns only the selected Profile's rows, pipeline, dues, alerts, and data-quality counts.
- Do not serialize bank, payment method, document, medical, disciplinary, payroll, peer performance, or other HR-only fields.

## Period and presentation checks

1. Preserve legal entity, inclusive source dates, and resolved-alert choice in the URL.
2. Reject invalid or reversed date ranges before the API call.
3. Confirm KPI summary, ranking row, trends, pipeline, upcoming collections, alerts, and drill-down use the same authorized Profile set and period contract.
   The KPI summary is calculated across the full authorized result, while only the already-ranked Staff rows are sliced by bounded `ranking_page` and `ranking_per_page`; pagination never recalculates rank or totals.
4. Pipeline stages, outstanding collection schedules, and intervention alerts return separate bounded page envelopes. Their page and page-size keys remain URL-preserved, out-of-range pages clamp to the last page, and changing one list page never changes authorized scope, ranking, KPI totals, or another list's current page. Pipeline aggregation is server-side; dues sort by due date then schedule ID; alerts sort by severity, detection time, then alert ID.
5. Display metric freshness and held-data warnings. Missing freshness is explicit and never represented as current.
6. Keep gross New Sales, signed commercial adjustments, net New Sales, current/prior booking collection and commission cohorts, and One-time/Long-term commission categories separate. New Sales target achievement and ranking use net, while qualifying booking count remains factual and is never reduced by a later value adjustment.
7. Treat a target as configured only when every calendar month touched by the requested inclusive period has exactly one approved value for that metric. Missing, partial, and overlapping approved configurations fail closed: the target amount, achievement percentage, variance, and display rank are `N/A`, never a partial denominator. A retained commercial adjustment without governed LKR evidence also withholds net actual, achievement and rank; it is never converted to zero.
8. Keep an approved zero target distinct from a missing target. Show its amount as zero and its achievement percentage as `N/A`; never divide by zero or treat zero as absent.
9. For a custom partial-month range, prorate each complete monthly target by inclusive calendar days. Quarter and year views sum their constituent monthly targets and do not carry unused target amounts into another month.
10. Display target state, target/actual/variance, configured-versus-expected month coverage, missing or ambiguous month starts, proration basis, and applied approved version lineage. Aggregate target totals remain `N/A` if any authorized Profile is incomplete or ambiguous, so pagination and missing configurations cannot overstate achievement.
11. Target evidence is performance-only. It must not create, change, approve, release, or recalculate commission or payroll facts.
12. Opening the current-period New Sales, New Booking count, New Customer count, eligible-collection, or earned-commission KPI calls the performance-owned KPI-fact endpoint with the dashboard legal entity, inclusive source dates, and selected Staff/Profile scope. The server reapplies current self/team/all authority and computes source amount/quantity over every matching signed fact at `occurred_at <= as_of` before pagination; no client total participates in authorization or calculation. New Sales accepts only the locked `new_business` classification and reconciles both gross `new_sales` and signed `new_sales_adjustment` facts to the net dashboard actual; adjustment quantity stays zero. New Booking count reads signed `new_sales` quantity without value adjustments; New Customer count reads signed `new_customer` quantity, including retained owner-correction debits and credits.
13. Collection cohort is report-relative, not the fact's booking-level business classification: resolve the canonical booking to its retained root attribution and compare root `secured_at` in the approved business timezone with the selected inclusive range. Inside the range is `current_period_booking`; before it is `prior_period_booking`. Missing/mismatched/future root lineage makes the split incomplete and blocks period close instead of being guessed. A generated recurring child uses its root contract date.
14. Commission category is the immutable fact dimension `one_time` or `long_term`, independent of collection cohort. The dashboard separately exposes current/prior booking commission and One-time/Long-term commission. Missing category makes only the category split incomplete; total signed earned commission remains visible.
15. Compare the returned source total with the selected dashboard value. New Sales additionally reconciles its qualifying-booking quantity; New Booking and New Customer cards reconcile their signed source quantity without treating their zero-valued metric rows as monetary KPIs. Cohort/category filter totals are calculated from the same full authorized source before slicing. A concurrent signed fact produces an explicit changed-KPI state and requires dashboard refresh; it is never silently labelled reconciled.
16. KPI source rows expose only Staff code, factual amount/quantity/date, source identity/checksum, the resolved cohort/category, and an allowlist of canonical booking/customer/receipt/schedule/commission/opportunity/task identifiers. Raw dimensions are withheld. New-customer evidence may expose an allowlisted customer ID but never customer contact fields. Booking Management remains separately guarded by `bookings.view`.
17. Every monthly trend value carries its frozen snapshot identity/version/cutoff. A complete New Sales card labels net as the headline and displays gross plus the signed adjustment separately. Opening New Sales, eligible collections, or commission calls the scoped snapshot-fact endpoint, which reapplies current self/team/all Profile scope, the frozen inclusive period, and `occurred_at <= cutoff`; the complete source amount/quantity must match the frozen authorized row total before the point is considered reconciled. Legacy frozen rows without explicit gross/adjustment/net fields and `new_sales_value_state=complete` remain incomplete until a governed rebuild; do not reinterpret their former gross `new_sales_lkr` column as net.
18. Frozen monthly rows retain current/prior collection and commission cohorts independently from One-time/Long-term commission category. Recompute each selected split from the complete authorized, cutoff-bound fact source before pagination and compare it only with the matching frozen marginal total. A legacy snapshot without completeness evidence or a source fact without root/category lineage returns `incomplete`, never zero or matched. Do not offer a cohort-by-category cross-tab until snapshots freeze that exact intersection.
19. Trend source facts expose only Staff code, factual amount/quantity/date, source identity/checksum, resolved cohort/category, and an allowlist of canonical booking/customer/receipt/schedule/commission/opportunity/task identifiers. Raw dimensions are withheld. Opening a canonical Booking Management record remains separately guarded by `bookings.view`; the Sales fact endpoint never grants booking authority.
20. Opening a pipeline stage calls the performance-owned pipeline-fact endpoint with the dashboard legal entity and selected Staff/Profile scope. Reconcile count, expected LKR, and probability-weighted LKR over all returned source rows before pagination. The response is a current stock with its own `as_of`; a changed dashboard point is explicit and requires refresh rather than being silently treated as reconciled. Opening the CRM workflow additionally requires `sales.crm.view` and rechecks direct-object scope.
21. Each outstanding collection schedule row uses the canonical source-currency equation `scheduled source amount - net source allocations = outstanding source amount`. Legacy rows fall back to `amount` and LKR only; a foreign-currency row requires its governed schedule LKR snapshot. Derive outstanding LKR proportionally from that frozen schedule basis, and withhold LKR when the snapshot is missing rather than treating source currency as LKR.
22. Opening a due row calls the performance-owned schedule-fact endpoint with the dashboard legal entity and selected Staff/Profile scope. It returns the complete source balance before paginated receipt-allocation evidence, exposes only canonical receipt IDs, and reports an explicit changed-balance or missing-LKR state. Booking Management remains separately guarded by `bookings.view`; dashboard access never grants booking or receipt authority.
23. Active portfolio is a current point-in-time stock and resolves exactly one approved, effective, legal-entity status-policy version. Missing or overlapping policy blocks the read; no source status is treated as active by convention. A selected past range returns `historical_reconstruction_required` until immutable booking-lifecycle reconstruction exists.
24. Reconcile the active count to distinct root attributions only, under the same current self/team/all or selected-Staff Profile scope. A row may be won, managed, or both in scope but is counted once. Fixed-term rows use the frozen contractual LKR value. A rolling-rule row is open-ended: lifetime value remains `N/A`, while monthly run rate, generated horizon/dates, collected allocations, and generated outstanding are reported separately and withheld when governed LKR evidence is missing.
25. Confirm the responsive cards, table alternative, loading, retry, error, empty, changed-point/mismatch, missing-conversion, keyboard, focus, and non-color states at 320px and larger widths.
26. Commission status cards keep stocks and flows separate. Pending means the current uncontested remainder on draft or pending-approval statements; approved means the current uncontested remainder on approved or partially-paid statements; held means unresolved held decisions, with eligible basis shown separately and never treated as payable commission; paid means confirmed payout allocations executed inside the selected business-date range, excluding reversed payouts. Current stock cards are available only when the range contains the current approved-business-timezone date. A historical range returns `N/A` until statement/hold event reconstruction is implemented; it never substitutes today's mutable status. Drill-down reapplies the same self/team/all or selected-Staff Profile scope and does not grant statement or payout permissions.
27. Outstanding and overdue collection cards are current stocks from non-superseded schedules minus net allocations, scoped to the current collection handler. Show Sales-target-eligible balances separately from the total customer account so security or other non-eligible schedules cannot inflate Sales collection work. Aging uses the approved business timezone and separates not due, due today, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 91+ days. A mixed/foreign-currency LKR total is complete only when every included schedule has a governed LKR basis; otherwise the monetary total is `N/A` while counts and source-currency breakdown remain evidence. Historical ranges return `N/A` until schedule and allocation event reconstruction exists. Booking links require the separate `bookings.view` permission.
28. Monthly trends select only the greatest frozen snapshot version for each legal-entity month so a rebuild cannot display two competing points. New Sales and collection target lines come from the same authorized frozen rows and preserve configured, approved-zero, missing, partial, and ambiguous states plus approved version lineage. Net New Sales direction requires explicit complete net evidence in both points. Month-over-month direction compares only consecutive calendar months with the identical authorized Profile set; the first point, a month gap, a changed scope, or incomplete net evidence is explicitly unavailable. A zero prior actual exposes the signed LKR delta but no percentage denominator. Direction never substitutes mutable current facts or silently compares different Staff populations.
29. Frozen collection aging reconstructs each schedule at the month-end business date using only schedule/revision evidence created by the snapshot cutoff, the effective revision boundary, net allocations allocated by month end and recorded by cutoff, and the latest collection-handler attribution event effective by month end and recorded by cutoff. Missing revision or handler lineage blocks close; it is never assigned from the mutable current projection.
30. Freeze not-due, due-today, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 91+ counts and governed LKR totals per Profile inside the KPI row snapshot. A missing foreign-currency LKR basis preserves counts and source-currency evidence but withholds the affected LKR aggregate. Legacy snapshots without the explicit aging contract remain unavailable until governed rebuild.
31. Opening a month-end aging point reapplies current self/team/all or selected-Staff scope, reconstructs the complete cutoff-bound source before pagination, and compares the normalized bucket/count/LKR contract with the frozen authorized rows. Rows expose schedule/booking IDs, booking number, due date, source-currency equation, bucket, eligibility and checksum only; customer contact and payment evidence are excluded, and Booking access remains separately permissioned.

## Deferred executable verification

During source implementation, do not run tests, builds, servers, migrations, queues, schedulers, or linters that execute project code. The consolidated verification stage must cover self, current manager, former manager, view-all, Accounts, HR, and peer identities; direct Staff, Profile, snapshot, opportunity, booking, receipt, and schedule IDs; company switching; URL reload and opportunity deep links; deactivation; missing/ambiguous Profile mapping; current KPI empty/signed-negative/boundary/concurrent-change and full-source-before-pagination reconciliation; month/quarter/year/custom cohort boundaries, recurring-child root lineage, missing/deleted/mismatched attribution, future-date anomaly, missing commission category, and category/cohort independence; source/LKR schedules, missing governed LKR snapshots, legacy LKR fallback, partial/net/over-allocation, superseded/deleted schedules, and concurrent allocation changes; empty, boundary, out-of-range, independently changed, concurrently changing, and dashboard-point-changed list pages; complete, missing, partial, overlapping, zero, full-month, partial-month, quarter, and year target scenarios; source reconciliation including post-cutoff corrections and superseded snapshots; and WCAG 2.2 AA evidence.

Migrations `2026_08_14_105000_align_sales_opportunity_soft_delete_contract.php` and `2026_08_14_106000_align_sales_booking_attribution_soft_delete_contract.php` add only nullable tombstone columns required by their mutable aggregate models. Preflight confirms each table exists and its column is absent. Migration `2026_08_15_103000_govern_net_new_sales_kpi_snapshots.php` leaves all legacy rows explicitly null/incomplete and refuses rollback after any new net-basis snapshot evidence exists. Migration `2026_08_31_150000_create_sales_portfolio_status_policy_versions.php` is additive and refuses rollback after any draft or approved policy evidence exists; disable the active-portfolio route/UI instead of deleting retained decisions. Frozen aging adds only retained JSON evidence to `metric_snapshot`; a source rollback disables its route/panel and stops adding new fields but never rewrites or deletes frozen rows. Source rollback removes the KPI/pipeline/trend/active-portfolio/aging fact endpoints and portal panels without altering Staff, Profile, KPI facts, alert, booking, collection, commission, CRM, or audit evidence.
