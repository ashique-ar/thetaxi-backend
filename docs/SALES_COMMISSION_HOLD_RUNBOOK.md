# Sales Commission Hold and Release Runbook

## Trigger, severity, and ownership

Use this runbook when an eligible confirmed receipt has a named commission hold, a release preview is blocked, a released amount is absent from a statement, or duplicate/concurrent release attempts are reported. Treat cross-legal-entity visibility, payout of a shadow/unconfirmed item, or source-row mutation as severity 1. Treat a stranded valid formula hold or statement projection delay as severity 2. Sales Operations owns triage; the separately authorized commission checker owns release; Finance owns liability and statement reconciliation; Security/Privacy owns scope incidents.

## Prerequisites and read-only diagnosis

1. Confirm the actor has an active internal Staff context, `sales.commission-decisions.view`, the required self/team/all scope, and—only for the append command—`sales.commission_holds.release`.
2. Read the immutable decision, receipt/component, attribution, beneficiary, eligible LKR/FX snapshot, frozen plan family, hold code, and event version. Do not infer missing identity, ownership, eligibility, finality, FX, or plan family.
3. Read the current release row by `commission_decision_id` and `idempotency_key`, relevant approved override/version/tier at the original receipt `received_at`, outbox event, metric fact, and any non-void statement line.
4. Use the no-write release preview. Preserve its blocker and checksum. A preview never authorizes payout and must report `write_performed: false`.

## Safe actions and approvals

- Only `rate_missing`, `override_ambiguous`, `plan_version_ambiguous`, `tier_missing_or_ambiguous`, and `formula_unsupported` may be replayed here, and only when approved configuration now resolves uniquely at the original receipt time.
- A commission-eligible pending-clearance or policy-missing receipt still creates exactly one immutable decision. Approved formula identifiers are frozen at the original receipt time; when policy forbids pre-final entitlement calculation, the amount remains null until confirmation. A failed finality event never releases it.
- A canonical transition to `failed` appends one checksummed `failed_finality_no_entitlement` resolution for every eligible receipt-component decision. The original hold remains immutable and the resolution is terminal and zero-value: it creates no commission metric, statement line, payout, or recovery. A replacement payment starts a new receipt/component/decision lineage; never reopen the failed decision.
- Only the canonical confirmed receipt-finality event may automatically release `cash_clearance_pending` when the original decision froze an approved policy that holds payout. The release freezes that event/evidence, requires the event's policy to equal the decision policy, uses the original formula identifiers and rounding, and is unique by both held decision and finality event. Every `policy_missing` or `pending_clearance` to `confirmed` transition must freeze the one approved original-time policy on its immutable event; missing, overlapping, unapproved, or same-maker/checker policy evidence blocks the transition.
- Shadow holds, disabled accrual, unconfirmed receipts, missing attribution/owner/beneficiary/eligibility/FX/finality/family, and still-ambiguous formulas remain blocked. Resolve them through their governed source workflow; never edit the commission decision.
- Use the queue's server-generated remediation contract rather than guessing from UI text. Formula/configuration holds may expose the preview; attribution/identity routes to the scoped attribution owner; Profile eligibility routes to Sales Profile administration; employment inactivity remains People Core-owned; cash clearance routes to canonical finality; and missing original FX evidence remains Booking Payment Ledger/Finance-owned with no fabricated correction link. Action paths are returned only when the current actor has the destination permission.
- A route to an owner is not permission to rewrite history. Attribution/Profile/policy changes affect only their governed effective periods. Historical non-formula entitlement requires a separately approved linked commission adjustment; until that source exists, the hold remains open and payout-blocked.
- The linked-adjustment command supports six evidence-complete hold variants across three correction types. `attribution_missing` requires exactly one active version-one canonical attribution subsequently created for the booking; its immutable version-one confirmation event must be actor-owned and effective no later than the receipt. `acquisition_profile_missing`, `acquisition_profile_ineligible`, and acquisition-side `legal_entity_mismatch` require exactly one actor-owned acquisition-owner correction effective at the original secured time, plus the actor-owned immutable event identifying the original receipt-time collection handler; the correction must replace the original missing/ineligible/wrong-entity owner and may not change the frozen booking, legal entity, secured time, plan family, or assignment. A Profile edit by itself never qualifies. A `legal_entity_mismatch` decision that already froze a beneficiary is beneficiary-side and remains blocked. `finality_policy_missing` requires one approved original-time policy plus a policy-linked `policy_missing` to `confirmed` event. Legacy `finality_policy_invalid` requires the same originally frozen invalid-payout policy plus its policy-linked `pending_clearance` to `confirmed` event; confirmation permits an adjustment but never legitimizes that policy while pending or permits a new invalid policy. Both require separate policy maker/checker and required private evidence. Every path must reconcile original eligible LKR/FX, acquisition eligibility, beneficiary Staff/Profile collection-and-commission eligibility, finality, and a positive original-time formula. Ambiguity, missing actors, unfrozen historical policy events, shadow decisions, or non-positive amounts remain blocked.
- Use `POST /api/sales/commission-earnings/{earning}/adjustment-preview` first. It performs no write and returns a checksum over the canonical attribution, receipt/finality, beneficiary Staff/Profile, plan, formula, tier/override, and original hold evidence. An actor with `sales.commission-recoveries.decide` may append only with that checksum, current decision version, reason, and fresh idempotency key. The attribution/finality evidence performer, policy maker/checker, and beneficiary cannot approve their corresponding adjustment. The resulting positive occurrence-period adjustment is unique per historical hold and enters metrics/statements once; it never changes the held decision or an earlier excluded statement line.
- `legal_entity_missing`, beneficiary-side `legal_entity_mismatch`, `beneficiary_missing`, `plan_family_missing`, collection/commission Profile eligibility, employment, FX, and `payment_finality_unknown` are not accepted by this command. They require separately designed canonical evidence and must not be forced through an implemented adjustment type. `payment_finality_failed` is never an entitlement adjustment; its governed ledger event creates only the terminal no-entitlement resolution above.
- The checker records a meaningful reason, current `expected_version`, and a fresh idempotency key. A retry uses the same key and identical evidence. A changed payload requires a new preview and key.
- Release appends one immutable row, outbox event, and metric fact. It does not change the held decision. The next eligible statement includes the release once as `commission_hold_release`; an earlier excluded hold line remains historical evidence.
- The permission is registered deny-by-default. Do not add it to a role bundle until the named business/Finance authorization decision is approved and recorded.
- Hold/release notifications are independently default-off. Do not enable `SALES_COMMISSION_NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED` until one effective, checksum-valid, maker-checker-approved in-app policy names the beneficiary recipient scope and approved content. Optional delivery requires the beneficiary's explicit enabled preference; missing or ambiguous policy, invalid checksum, missing preference, inactive employment/user/Profile, or changed scope remains visibly blocked or cancelled. No scheduler cadence has been invented: preview `sales:process-commission-notifications` and use `--commit` only through an approved operational run. Email/SMS/provider delivery remains unavailable.
- Notification diagnosis uses `sales_commission_notification_deliveries` and its append-only delivery events. Never decrypt rendered payloads for routine triage. The delivered database payload may contain only approved title/body, event type, and `/sales/commission-holds`; booking/customer/amount/beneficiary and peer identifiers are prohibited. Revalidate the current internal beneficiary before every attempt, and treat any peer or former-manager delivery as severity 1.

## Reconciliation and recovery

Reconcile eligible receipt-component-to-decision cardinality, decision-to-release/adjustment/terminal-resolution exclusivity, snapshot checksum, initial/current receipt finality, frozen policy and finality event/evidence, canonical attribution version, acquisition-correction event, original handler event, beneficiary/legal entity, approved rule IDs, rounded amount, one release or adjustment metric fact, one non-void statement claim, statement gross/net/carry-forward, outbox delivery, notification source-event uniqueness, policy/preference snapshot, payload checksum, attempts, cancellation, and database notification ID. Failed finality requires exactly one resolution bound to its event and zero financial projections. A duplicate, failed/unconfirmed finality, wrong-scope, stale-version, changed-preview, self-approval, missing-rule, or ambiguous-tier attempt must leave no release/adjustment row or financial projection.

Do not delete or update a release or linked adjustment. If the calculated entitlement later proves wrong, use the existing linked refund/recovery or correction workflow. The late-finality migration may roll back only before any linked finality adjustment or policy-frozen receipt-finality event exists; otherwise export/reconcile and use forward remediation. Other additive release or notification tables may be removed only while empty. Record incident timeline, actors, correlation/idempotency IDs, affected legal entity/Staff, reconciled amount, communications, approval, and post-incident actions.
